# Automatic stream recovery, 2026-10-10

Implemented and installed recovery in host-local EpicVM stream viewers. New imported streams receive the same module through provision_direct_stream.py; the supervisor patches existing routes.

## Behavior

- Connection loss shows a recovery panel explaining that Windows apps stay running.
- Offline status pauses automatic retries; returning online resumes monitoring.
- Closed WebSocket or failed/disconnected WebRTC gets an eight-second grace period, followed by reconnecting to the same host/application.
- Startup without video and a supported WebRTC receiver with a stalled frame counter are detected after thirty seconds. Hidden tabs get fresh grace time when visible again.
- Automatic recovery is limited to three attempts with backoff. Only thirty seconds of healthy connectivity resets that budget.
- A manual Try reconnecting button starts a fresh budget; busy guards prevent repeated clicks from creating overlapping attempts.
- Reconnect bounds old-stream teardown and closes its WebSocket and transport before creating a new stream. It never creates, stops, or recreates a Windows seat.
- After exhausted attempts, the panel offers retry and Back to workspace. Leaving disposes monitoring, so intentional navigation does not reconnect.
